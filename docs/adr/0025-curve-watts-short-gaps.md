# Curve watts follow Lifetime across long Sample gaps

Plant view’s curve hair shows curve watts, not Power. Per Logger, consecutive Samples at most 15 minutes apart interpolate linearly so a one-minute offset or a missed snapshot does not look like a dead Logger. A longer gap where Lifetime energy still rose (delta above 0.02 kWh and implied average watts at least max(20 W, 40% of the quieter endpoint Power)) draws as a smooth Power curve — that energy happened. A longer gap where Lifetime barely moved valleys (dotted); the hair dashes that Logger; Plant omits it. A failed parse does not close a gap. Plant is the sum of Loggers that have curve watts then (smooth or near), never a held silent Logger, never a Logger in fail, never an interpolated Plant-only polyline. Snap-only readout, interpolating silence, and interpolating Plant were rejected: the first makes normal cadence look gappy, the second invents production, the third hides which Logger failed.
